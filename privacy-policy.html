Privacy Policy

How enquiry information is handled on this website.

Draft for review — November 2026

1. Introduction

Discover Travel Club respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This draft must be checked against actual operating practices before publication.

2. Information collected

Enquiry forms may collect your name, email address, telephone number, areas of interest and message.

3. Use of information

Information is used to respond to requests, provide information about services requested and maintain appropriate business records. Do not submit payment card details through this website.

4. Sharing, security and retention

Relevant information may be shared with service providers where necessary to respond to an enquiry, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law. Reasonable security measures should be used, and information retained only as required.

5. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information or raise a concern about its handling.

6. Complete before launch

Add the responsible party's legal name, address and Information Officer details. Have this policy reviewed for POPIA compliance before publication. Contact us through the Contact Us page.
