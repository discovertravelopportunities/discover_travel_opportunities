# Discover Travel Club — standalone business website

## Local demo
Open `index.html` directly, or run `python -m http.server 8000` in this folder and visit `http://localhost:8000`.
When opened directly from disk, forms validate and save demo submissions to this browser's local storage only. They do not send email.

## Free hosting with Netlify
1. Create a free Netlify account and choose Add new site → Deploy manually.
2. Upload the contents of this folder.
3. Ensure Netlify Forms detection is enabled. The enquiry forms use Netlify's standard HTML form handling.
4. Submit a test form. In Site configuration → Forms, configure an email notification.
5. Add your domain in Domain management and follow the DNS instructions.

## Before launch
- Replace the typographic logo placeholder with the official Discover Travel Club logo (no original logo file was available in this build context).
- Confirm the public enquiry email currently displayed as `info@discovertravelclub.co.za`.
- Add approved membership pricing, inclusions, durations and conditions; these have not been invented.
- Add current travel offers, destinations and event details.
- Complete the Privacy Policy with the responsible legal entity, address and Information Officer details; obtain POPIA review.
- Have Website Terms & Conditions reviewed by a South African legal professional.
- Hero and destination photography loads from Unsplash and needs an internet connection. Replace with owned/licensed images if preferred.
- Travel savings up to 70% are subject to availability and conditions.
- Lifestyle Concierge services are optional, separately quoted and paid; they are not included in standard membership fees.
- No timeshare exit or financial outcome is guaranteed.
